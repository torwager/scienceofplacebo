Researcher network

Researchers with at least 8 papers in the database, shown as a map. Co-authorship links two researchers who have published together (thicker means more shared papers); similarity links researchers who study similar things, judged from the keyword tags and free keywords of their papers. Drag nodes, zoom with the buttons or the scroll wheel, hover over a node or link for details, and click a node to pin its details while you browse its papers. The keyword menu rebuilds the map from only the papers carrying that tag.

Node size = papers in the database · link width = co-authored papers
