Science of Placebo is a shared resource for people who study, practise, or are curious about placebo and nocebo effects. Here is what you can do now, and what a free account will add.

★

Keep a list of papers

Star any paper on the feed, in the database or on its page. "My list" collects them, filters the feed and database to them, exports them, and copies citations. Available now, no account needed; the list lives in your browser.

Discuss

Comment on any paper and take part in the discussion board. Posting uses a GitHub account, which is free and takes a minute to create.

With a free account, your stars are saved across devices and count as nominations. The most-nominated papers form a community-curated reading list, open to everyone.

Sign in

Signing in uses the same GitHub identity as comments. The site stores only your GitHub user id, login and the ids of the papers you star, never a password or email. Your browser list is merged into the account the first time you sign in.
