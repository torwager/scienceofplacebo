JIPS — the Journal of Interdisciplinary Placebo Studies database. Katja Weimer, Paul Enck and colleagues hand-curated monthly lists of new placebo and nocebo publications from 2016 to 2025 and maintain a library of more than 5,000 papers. Science of Placebo is inspired by that work and was seeded with their monthly lists. Visit jips.online ↗
